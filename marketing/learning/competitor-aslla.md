## Assessment (inference)
- Threat level: TW/HK **high** (Aslla holds the exact name 上次是什麼時候, plus 5+ others with the same phrase). KR **high**. JP **medium**: Aslla is weak there, but Mogami and Sarun are entrenched and 「最後にやった」 is crowded (Itsu, なんにちぶり, LastTap). US **medium-low**: Aslla has just 1 rating; the real rivals are Sarun ($5.99) and the free apps.
- The category is clearly proven (lots of 2026 entrants, KR/TW chart success), but it's saturated. Our edge has to be positioning, not features: Aslla has already shipped widgets, Lock Screen logging, photos and the timeline — none of which Been Ages has (dev confirmed 2026-10-20, d3a510c). Don't compete on those; lead with reminders (flexible schedules, snooze, calendar export), on-device privacy (no account/server/tracking), per-item history, custom categories, zh/en/ja. AI input is 'Coming soon' only.
- Price: Aslla matches $2.99 in the US. Our "10 items free (10 records each)" works as a try-before-you-buy edge against their paid-upfront model.

1. Chinese name: don't use 上次是什麼時候. Pick something distinctive (e.g. 好久沒… / 多久沒做了) and keep that phrase only in keywords or the subtitle. **Needs Chun's decision.**
2. JP name: "Been Ages - 最後にやった日" collides with なんにちぶり？ - 最後にやった日の記録. Consider 「何日ぶり」 or 「いつぶり」 wording, but avoid copying いつやった？ or Itsu. **Needs Chun's decision.**

4. Market priority: US first (weakest incumbents, English launch on PH and X), then JP with a distinctive name. Keep TW light, and deprioritize KR (Aslla is strong there). **Confirm with Chun.**
5. Positioning: "free to try (10 items, 10 records each), then pay once, no subscription" vs paid-upfront and ad-supported rivals. Use "pay once" / "one-time purchase" wording only (banned word rule).
