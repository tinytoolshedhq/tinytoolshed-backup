# MX phrase bank (México)
> ⚠️ **Machine-compiled, not checked by a native speaker.** Rebuilt 2026-10-20 (Taipei), read-only research of public X posts. Bluesky has very little Mexican Spanish content for this topic.

## Natural phrasing
- **"¿Cada cuánto cambian ___?"** (ustedes form) is the native question: "Cada cuánto cambian el cepillo de dientes?" ([X @libroderimas](https://x.com/libroderimas/status/2102753946808832387)); "ABRO DEBATE: cada cuanto cambian sus sábanas/pijamas??" — **116 replies, 163 likes** ([X @lilitthiie](https://x.com/lilitthiie/status/2102552310488719538)).
- **"Pago único, sin suscripción"** is the standard pairing in app copy: "Pago único, sin suscripción." ([X @chironex78](https://x.com/chironex78/status/2108845401977589941)), "US$19 pago único · Sin suscripción" ([X @RaulBerrio33526](https://x.com/RaulBerrio33526/status/2107155274515747243)). Our line: **"Pago único, sin suscripción, sin cuenta."**
- Use Mexican usage: **ustedes / tú**, "celular" (not "móvil"), MXN where prices appear. Not vosotros, not vos. Note many "pago único" posts found were from Spain (€, "móvil") — don't copy their vocabulary.

## High-engagement patterns
- **"ABRO DEBATE:" + everyday frequency question** (sheets, toothbrush). The sábanas thread above is the strongest example found.
- Short, lowercase, jokey questions; replies answer with a number or a confession.

## Things to avoid
- **"Un solo pago de por vida"** — re-verified: the most recent match is spam for a Telegram group selling leaked influencer content ("Promoción del mes  Un solo pago de por vida  Grupo de telegram…", [X @Daniel41043348](https://x.com/Daniel41043348/status/2109497765113802796)). Avoid entirely.
- "desbloquear" (= unlock; brand rule).
- Spain-isms: vosotros, "móvil", "vale", € prices.
- AI-generated promo style ("Tu cara. Tu fantasía…", "Enlace en la bio", hashtag stacks) — the "pago único, sin suscripción" phrase is also used by these accounts, so pair it with a human, specific sentence.

## 本週學習（2026-10-26，Taipei）— 機器整理，未經母語者校對
Read-only pass over X (past 7 days) and Bluesky (sort=top, since 2026-10-19). **Caveat:** Spanish-language search is dominated by Argentina and Spain; only posts with a Mexican location or clearly Mexican slang are treated as MX tone below.

1. **"Recomienden apps…" is the natural MX/LatAm ask, short and casual.** "Recomienden apps chidasss🤠" ([X @miax77_](https://x.com/miax77_/status/2114429086844006676)); "Recomienden apps para conocer gente #cdmx" ([X @Oscargonzam9](https://x.com/Oscargonzam9/status/2113891322130227607)); and the free-first filter: "recomienden apps para ver animes pero k no sean esas k se pagan" ([X @h91735](https://x.com/h91735/status/2112028308514693542), 2 replies). **Use:** in replies, say clearly what is free (10 cosas gratis) before mentioning the one-time price.
2. **Subscription fatigue is framed as "otra suscripción" + annoyance, not ideology.** "Es estúpido pagar Amazon prime video en su versión sin anuncios y que … salga con otra suscripción. Que fastidio." ([X @evicede](https://x.com/evicede/status/2113333106254246008), México DF); "Me molestan mucho los anuncios de Yt 😩, pero no pienso pagar otra suscripción 😢" ([X @Shineulight](https://x.com/Shineulight/status/2113503858924138934)); "Voy a tener que pagar otra suscripcion" (49 likes, [X @gastonfonceadev](https://x.com/gastonfonceadev/status/2112284406845145303), not MX-located). **Use:** "sin otra suscripción" / "pagas una vez y ya" reads more natural than "pago único".
3. **The relatable form is a confession, not a question:** "No me acuerdo cuándo fue la última vez que…" ("…dormí bien", "…dormí 8 horas"; MX example: "No me acuerdo cuando fue la última vez que le di dislike a un video… creo que fue el YouTube Rewind 2018 🤔" [X @alvarogarciiaYT](https://x.com/alvarogarciiaYT/status/2113576928393273748), Coahuila). "Hace cuánto no…" showed up almost only from Argentine accounts; don't use it as the MX default. Direct "¿Cuándo fue la última vez que…?" questions got near-zero replies this week unless emotional (te amo / te quisieron tanto).
