# US phrase bank (United States)
Rebuilt 2026-10-20 (Taipei) from fresh read-only research of public X (x namespace search) and Bluesky (public search API). No posting, replying, liking, following or logging in. Quotes are verbatim excerpts; reply/like counts as of 2026-10-20.

## Natural phrasing
- **"It's been a minute"** = "it's been a while". Very common, casual: "It's been a minute since I played" ([X @Chanellly_](https://x.com/Chanellly_/status/2106575244093960428)), "It's been a minute since I've been on here." ([X @SourceElemmvy](https://x.com/SourceElemmvy/status/2111554365307888103)). Americans understand "ages" fine — keep the app name Been Ages, mix "a minute" into body copy.
- **"When was the last time you changed your ___?"** is the natural question form (toothbrush, air filter, furnace filter, cabin filter).
- **"Pay once"** phrasing in the wild: "Pay once, no subscription." ([X @not_sacke](https://x.com/not_sacke/status/2109328524683206954)); "i would rather pay once for a utility app and forget about it" ([X @Bagdex](https://x.com/Bagdex/status/2105285701311070372)); "No subscriptions. No ads. No data harvesting. … Just pay once and own the app forever." ([X @NickHirras](https://x.com/NickHirras/status/2106544802967756992)).
- **"Everything is a subscription now."** Verbatim on Bluesky: "Everything is a subscription now. Just bleeding your wallet dry 1 dollar at a time." ([bsky @tg2](https://bsky.app/profile/tg2.bsky.social/post/3mxwvs62gg227)). Related grievance: "Nameberry is now just another subscription...that I won't pay for." ([bsky @dawnlysimple](https://bsky.app/profile/dawnlysimple.bsky.social/post/3mxofwycfpk2p)); "Enshittification." as a one-word reply to subscription creep ([bsky @rebelb1ue](https://bsky.app/profile/rebelb1ue.bsky.social/post/3mxr65e3tqc24), 17 likes).

## High-engagement patterns
1. **Plain "when was the last time" + a nudge to go check.** Best example found: "When was the last time you changed the air filter in your home please go check if you haven't in a while" — **84 replies, 7,030 likes** ([X @wobblymami](https://x.com/wobblymami/status/2103193180695151063)). Toothbrush versions from individuals: 33 replies ([X @Oluwabunminire](https://x.com/Oluwabunminire/status/2104805874875371574)), 20 replies ([X @ChildHealthhero](https://x.com/ChildHealthhero/status/2104809354192888099)), 70 replies ([X @Millyivie](https://x.com/Millyivie/status/2109625363253075995)).
2. **"Be honest… No judgment."** Still a common format ("Writers, be honest… Drop your number. No judgment here!" [bsky @seunnotes](https://bsky.app/profile/seunnotes.bsky.social/post/3mxh3s2bdec2a); "Be honest… when was the last time you actually changed your toothbrush…" [X @collegedental42](https://x.com/collegedental42/status/2105578516766933319)). **Re-verification caveat:** this round the examples found were mostly brand/bot accounts with 0–1 replies, and the phrase is heavily used by NSFW and trading-promo accounts. The earlier "big reply counts" finding did NOT re-verify this round; the reply-driving part is the concrete everyday question, not the "be honest" tag. Keep it short and personal, and don't lean on it as the hook alone.
3. **Subscription fatigue** reliably gets agreement on Bluesky; pay-once indie apps get "rooting for u" replies on X ([X @lazeskid](https://x.com/lazeskid/status/2111415454132850818)).

## Things to avoid
- "unlock" (brand rule; also reads like paywall/NSFW spam: "Image unlocks in the browser").
- Dental-office / HVAC-ad voice ("Dental Tip:", "Schedule your appointment!", phone numbers, hashtag stacks). Many low-engagement posts are exactly this; sound like a person, not a clinic.
- Hype/LLM voice ("game-changer 🚀", "Let that sink in", "In today's rapidly evolving…").
- "Lifetime access" — associated with subscription apps selling LTDs; say "pay once" / "one time".
- Widgets, iCloud backup, Family Sharing, launch discounts (product decisions).

## 本週學習（2026-10-26，Taipei）
Read-only pass over X (past 7 days, relevancy search) and Bluesky (public search API, sort=top, since 2026-10-19). No posting, replying, liking or following. Counts are replies/likes as of 2026-10-26.

1. **Subscription anger is sharpest when it's about a tiny, simple app.** Best Been Ages-adjacent quote of the week: "I miss when apps used to be one time fees. Everything is a fucking subscription now. There are book logging apps that want $50+ a year. I have a notebook I've been using for almost 10 years. I don't need you!" ([bsky @chrismartinez.latinsky.app](https://bsky.app/profile/chrismartinez.latinsky.app/post/3myphecvavc2c), 1/9). Same shape: "A photo collage app I've had for nearly a decade now requires a subscription. TO PUT 4 PHOTOS IN A GRID." ([bsky @dschnoeb](https://bsky.app/profile/dschnoeb.bsky.social/post/3myiafpm4dk2y)); "I feel so old. I don't need another subscription 👵" ([bsky @becadreads](https://bsky.app/profile/becadreads.bsky.social/post/3myhu7giqlk2y), 5/27). **Use:** name the small job ("it just counts days") and contrast it with the subscription, rather than saying "no subscription" in the abstract. A reply to posts like these should agree first and only then mention we're pay-once.
2. **"One-time purchase. No subscription." is now wallpaper on indie-dev X.** A dozen launches this week used the exact line and nearly all got 0–3 likes (e.g. [X @domrealmapps](https://x.com/domrealmapps/status/2113081409175007685), [X @CHRONELLEARC](https://x.com/CHRONELLEARC/status/2112571830305517992)). The one that read human started with a person: "My sister asked me for an app to track her expenses. So I built one 😊" ([X @didacsd](https://x.com/didacsd/status/2114490118744473626)). **Use:** open with the small real-life reason, put "pay once" near the end.
3. **"When was the last time you…" still works best as a short, concrete, slightly nostalgic question.** Top-engaged examples were nostalgia/observation, not self-improvement: "firefighters rescuing cats from trees. Seriously when was the last time you saw that happen? Must be years." ([bsky @ricardoautobahn.co.uk](https://bsky.app/profile/ricardoautobahn.co.uk/post/3mymdzekn7s2w), 9/15); "When was the last time you stopped to help someone with a busted fan belt…" ([bsky @perreaux](https://bsky.app/profile/perreaux.bsky.social/post/3mymnngf67k2h), 3/17); "When was the last time you went to the dentist BE HONESTTTTTT!!!!!" ([X @ItsLethalTTV](https://x.com/ItsLethalTTV/status/2114532212473229327)). Stacked multi-question posts did OK only from an established account ([X @AltCulture](https://x.com/AltCulture/status/2113087044578869384), 5/33). **Caveat:** the bare phrase is heavily used by NSFW and crypto accounts this week; always attach a specific everyday object (dentist, air filter, a whole album).
