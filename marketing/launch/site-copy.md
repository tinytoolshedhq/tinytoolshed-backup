## 3. Been Ages page

# Been Ages: Last Time Tracker
### Days since you last did it.

When did you last change your toothbrush? Clean the fridge? Water the plants?
If the answer is "uh… it's been ages," this app is for you.

**How it works**
1. Add the things you want to keep track of.
2. Tap when you do one.
3. See how many days it's been — at a glance.

**Good for**
- Chores: bedding, fridge, oven, filters
- Self-care: haircuts, toothbrush, contact lenses
- Home & car: boiler service, car service
- People: last time you called someone

**Features**
- Reminders when it's due: every N days, weeks, months or years, on a fixed weekday or day of the month, at the time you choose. Snooze, or add the next date to your calendar.
- Your own categories.
- Full history for every item.
- In English, Chinese and Japanese, following your iPhone's language.
- Back up by exporting a file and importing it later (manual; no iCloud sync).
- *Coming soon:* type or speak to add items, ask "how long since…", say "just did it", and undo. Runs fully on your iPhone, offline.

**Pricing**
- Free: track up to **10 items, 10 records per item**.
- One-time purchase (Pro) — US $2.99: **unlimited items**, unlimited records per item, and 3 extra share card colors (mint, sky, blush; cream is free). No subscription. Family Sharing is not available.

**Privacy**
Been Ages stores your items only on your device. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking.

## 4. Privacy Policy (draft)

**Privacy Policy**
Last updated: [DATE]

This policy covers apps made by Tiny Toolshed, including Been Ages ("the app").

**What we collect**
We don't collect personal data. Been Ages has no account and no server. The items and dates you enter are stored only on your device, and we never receive them.

**Backups**
The app does not sync to iCloud or any server. You can export a backup file yourself and import it later; where you save that file is up to you. If your iPhone itself backs up to iCloud, that is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.

**Analytics and crash reports**
The app does not track you and does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If you've opted in to share diagnostics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us.

**Purchases**
In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

**Email**
If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We don't share it.

**Children**
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

**Deleting your data**
Deleting the app deletes all your data; you can also delete individual items in the app. Deleted items go to Settings > Recently Deleted, where you can restore them or delete them permanently.

**Changes**
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

**Contact**
tinytoolshedhq@gmail.com

## 6. Support

**Support**

Need help with Been Ages or another Tiny Toolshed app?
Email **tinytoolshedhq@gmail.com** — we usually reply within [TODO: X] days.

**Quick answers**
- **Can my family share my purchase?** No. Family Sharing is not available for Been Ages.
- **I paid but I'm back on the free limit (10 items, 10 records per item).** Open the purchase screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
- **How do I get a refund?** Refunds go through Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
- **Will my data move to a new phone?** There's no iCloud sync. Export a backup file on your old phone and import it on the new one.
- **Is there a widget?** Not at the moment.
- **How do I delete my data?** Deleting the app deletes all your data; you can also delete individual items in the app (they go to Settings > Recently Deleted, where you can restore or purge them).
